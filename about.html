About Me

I hold a Bachelor of Science in Biology from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, followed by a PhD in Molecular Biology and Biochemistry from the University of Nottingham. I further pursued a Master’s degree in Bioinformatics at Birkbeck, University of London.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of working with clinical data from three major hospitals — in Athens, San Francisco, and Cambridge — contributing to projects spanning oncology, genomics, and systems biology.

My academic and professional focus gravitates toward the application of novel computational technologies, innovative data visualisation, and the exploration of clinical datasets. I deeply enjoy every stage of the computational pipeline — from building databases and automating pipelines, to creating useful tools, analysing omics data, and publishing impactful research.

Yet, the true reward lies in those moments when wet lab findings elegantly align with bioinformatic predictions — when the tentacles of uncertainty briefly part, and something coherent stares back.

While I welcome statistical rigor, I find the fetishisation of p-values rather unpleasant. Context, creativity, and curiosity are often far more enlightening.
